Tier 1: Executive Super Admin (Company / Brand Headquarters)
Target: Platform Owners, C-Suite & Compliance Head

Core Operational Powers

  • Master Pricing Matrix: Controls wholesale service prices, MRP ceilings, and minimum retailer margins for GST, ITR, PAN & Certificates.
  • Bank UTR Verification: Reviews and approves/rejects distributor NEFT/RTGS/IMPS/UPI deposit proofs to fund master wallets.
  • Tenant & System Governance: Oversees tenant white-labeling, WhatsApp Cloud API keys, and multi-region database rules.

Security & Fraud Controls

  • Double-Entry Ledger Audit: Access to immutably hashed financial ledger records with actor tracking.
  • Account Safeguards: Can suspend, activate, or reassign rogue distributor/outlet branches instantly.
  • Isolation: Strictly holds sovereign administrative authority over company finances.
Tier 2: Enterprise Master Distributor (Regional Network Partner)
Target: District Business Heads & FinTech Master Franchises

Core Operational Powers

  • Downline Network Expansion: Onboards, verifies, and activates new Tier 3 Retailer Outlets within designated territories.
  • Float Allocation Engine: Purchases bulk liquidity from HQ and dispatches instant wallet top-ups to subordinate retail outlets.
  • Automated Commission Split: Earns passive wholesale commission overrides credited instantly upon every downline filing.

Security & Fraud Controls

  • Territory Confinement: Strictly restricted to viewing downline stores; zero visibility into peer distributors or parent HQ settings.
  • Credit Guard: Cannot extend float beyond available prepaid balance, preventing unsecured company credit exposure.
  • Approval Chain: Can request top-up from HQ but cannot self-approve deposits.

Tier 3: Commercial Retailer Outlet (Store Owner / Point of Sale Center)
Target: Local Tax Consultancies, CSC Desks & Retail Outlets

Core Operational Powers

  • Counter Staff Management: Creates, monitors, and provisions login credentials for store clerks (Tier 4 Operators).
  • Store Liquidity Management: Submits Float Top-Up requests to their parent Distributor; monitors store opening & closing balance.
  • Store Revenue Retention: Retains direct retail margin (Customer Fee minus Wholesale Base) on every completed filing.

Security & Fraud Controls

  • Data Isolation: Store owner cannot view client data, orders, or revenues of neighboring competitor retailers.
  • Staff Surveillance: Real-time visibility into every filing submitted and float spent by counter clerks.
  • No Upstream Access: Strictly blocked from distributor or super-admin dashboards.
Tier 4: Frontline Counter Operator (Store Employee / Filing Clerk)
Target: Service Desk Staff, Data Entry Clerks & Cashiers

Core Operational Powers

  • Customer Intake & Filing: Gathers client documents, files GST returns (GSTR-1/3B), ITR (1/2/4), and PAN applications.
  • AI-Assisted Copilot: Uses AI OCR to auto-read Aadhaar, PAN, and Form 16 without manual typing errors.
  • Cash Collection & Invoicing: Receives customer payment over counter and generates official printed tax receipts with QR validation.

Strict Frontline Guardrails

  • Hidden Financials: Zero visibility into store profit margins, distributor commissions, or wholesale pricing.
  • No Withdrawal Authority: Cannot transfer wallet funds outside or alter service pricing.
  • Shift Confinement: Tied strictly to the store wallet assigned by their store manager.

Why Enterprise Clients Adopt This 4-Tier Governance Model:

1. Zero Revenue Leakage: Double-entry ACID wallet verification deducts fees and credits commissions instantaneously with zero reconciliation delay.
2. Employee Proof Security: Frontline operators (Tier 4) can process high-volume tax filings efficiently using AI OCR, but are completely air-gapped from store profit data and cannot misappropriate funds.
3. Scalable Distribution: Master distributors operate as autonomous regional hubs, allowing the platform to scale to thousands of retail stores across India without administrative bottlenecks.
